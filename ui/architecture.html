Multi-Agent PPO (CTDE)
PROVEN DEFENSE
  • Dynamic Co-evolution: Attacker continually probes vulnerabilities, forcing the Defender to generalize against zero-day payloads.
  • Centralized Critic V_phi(S): Observes global environment state (attacker intent + task + environment state) to provide low-variance Generalized Advantage Estimation (GAE).
  • PPO Clipped Objective with KL Penalty: Regularizes updates around pi_SFT to preserve benign task utility while maximizing injection resistance.
  • Decentralized Execution: At test/inference time, the Defender operates strictly on its local observation, with zero runtime critic overhead.
Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT)
STATIC BASELINE
  • Passive Offline Dataset: Trained solely via Cross-Entropy on fixed prompt-completion pairs.
  • Zero Active Exploration: Cannot anticipate unseen adversarial distributions, novel encodings, or recursive obfuscation.
  • OOD Vulnerability: Fails on 12.8% of novel/out-of-distribution attack variants in empirical testing (ASR = 12.78%).
  • Overfitting Trap: Achieves 100% on in-distribution templates but exhibits brittle boundary collapse under distribution shift.

Mathematical Game Formulation & Optimization Objectives
1. Defender Policy PPO Objective with KL Reference Anchor:
L^{CLIP}(theta) = E_t [ min(r_t(theta) * A_hat_t, clip(r_t(theta), 1 - epsilon, 1 + epsilon) * A_hat_t) ] - beta_KL * D_KL(pi_theta || pi_SFT) + c_e * H(pi_theta)
where r_t(theta) = pi_theta(a_t|s_t) / pi_{theta_old}(a_t|s_t), A_hat_t is computed via GAE(lambda), and pi_SFT acts as an anchor to prevent catastrophic forgetting.

2. Centralized Critic Objective:
L^V(phi) = 0.5 * E_t [ (V_phi(S_t) - R_t^{target})^2 ]
where S_t = (s_t^{atk}, s_t^{def}, s_t^{env}) encapsulates the full joint state of the cyber environment.

3. Static SFT Baseline Objective (Passive Cross-Entropy):
L^{SFT}(theta) = -sum_{(x_i, y_i) in D_static} log P_theta(y_i | x_i)
Lacks active exploration: bounded by fixed training distribution, leading to out-of-distribution failure.
